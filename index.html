The game is divided into 7 shifts. During each shift, you serve customers like in a cashier sim: find what they want, ring up their order and give them the right change.

Of course, they also talk. Conversations with customers and the newspaper are where all the storylines unfold.

Between customers, you'll sometimes get breaks you can spend on different puzzle minigames. Their solutions may become useful later or lead to new interactions.

You don't have to solve everything or remember every detail to reach the end of the game. Follow what catches your interest.

Click on an item to read its description. Drag it into the bag to add it to the purchase.

To ring up the total, enter the price on the till, then drag the lever down.

Work out the customer's change, then click on the right banknotes and coins to give it to them.

Click on the [...] button to talk to the customer or ask them to remind or explain their order.

Dialogue options marked with * must be chosen before you can finish the scene.

During breaks, you can read the newspaper and solve puzzles.

You can choose one puzzle per break. Once you open one, your choice is final for that break.

You can skip a break or give up on a puzzle midway, but keep in mind that you may not get enough breaks to solve every puzzle later.

Wordoku works like Sudoku, but uses letters instead of numbers.

Fill the grid so that every row, column and inner square contains each available letter exactly once.

Once the grid is complete, look for hidden words and mark them by clicking their first and last letters.

Follow the instructions line by line and type them directly on your keyboard.

The 1), 2), 3)... at the beginning mark new lines and aren't typed. Every other number tells you how many times to press the key that follows it: 2sp means two spaces, 3bsp means three backspaces, 2_ means two underscores, and so on.

sp means Space and bsp means Backspace.

Press Enter after finishing each line.

A few mistakes are fine as long as you can still recognise the picture.

To try giving the tooth to someone, drag it into the bag along with their normal order, then ring up the purchase as usual.

Some customers may notice the tooth right away. Others may take it with them and only notice after leaving the shop, so you won't always know the outcome immediately.

Talk to people, listen for clues and your heart, and slip it into an order when you think you know who should receive it.

Watch the crosses first. They show the pattern of gaps you need to hit between the fingers. You'll have to repeat the same pattern three times in the same order to complete each round.

The respectometer falls while you hesitate and rises when you hit the right gaps. Deeper hits, closer to the palm, raise it more.

Hitting the hand costs a heart. Running out of hearts ends the attempt.

Complete all three rounds with enough respect to impress Lispy Kid.

Dialogue box: press Enter or Space to continue or choose an option. Use the Up and Down arrow keys to select an option.

Your progress is saved automatically at the end of each shift. Saves are stored locally in your browser.

The game may look blurry on high-DPI displays or with scaling above 100%.
