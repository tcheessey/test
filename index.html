Each shift is made up of customers and breaks.

Serve customers, talk to them and (if you want) pay attention to what they tell you. Some things you learn may be useful later.

During breaks, you can spend time on puzzles from the newspaper or elsewhere. Solving a puzzle may give you information or clues that can be used elsewhere in the game.

Not everything has to be completed or discovered to finish a shift.

Click on an item to read its description. Drag it into the bag to add it to the purchase.

To ring up the total, enter the price on the till, then drag the lever down.

Count the customer's money, then click on the right banknotes and coins to give them their change.

Click on the [...] button to talk to the customer or ask them to remind or explain their order.

Dialogue branches marked with * must be read through to finish the scene.

Most shifts have one or two breaks between customers.

You can choose one activity during each break.

Once you open a puzzle or activity, your choice is final for that break. You can't close it and choose another one instead.

There are almost enough breaks to try every puzzle in the game, so ideally, don't skip them.

Wordoku works like Sudoku, but uses letters instead of numbers.

Fill the grid so that every row, column and marked area contains each available letter exactly once.

Once the grid is complete, look through the finished grid for hidden words.

A number followed by a bracket, like 1) or 2), means new line. Other numbers mean how many times to hit that key — 2sp is two spaces, 3bsp is three backspaces, 2_ is two underscores, same with other symbols.

Just do it in order and the picture appears.
